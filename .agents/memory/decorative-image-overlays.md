---
name: Decorative image overlays
description: How to place uploaded artwork across adjacent sections with different background colors.
---

When uploaded artwork must cross from a white area into a colored section, use a trimmed PNG with the outer background removed and align its visible bounds to the section edge.

**Why:** Keeping the original white canvas makes the image look clipped or leaves a white rectangle between the artwork and the contrasting section.

**How to apply:** Remove only the contiguous outer background, preserve interior highlights, trim transparent margins, and let the image overflow above the section rather than clipping it at the boundary.
